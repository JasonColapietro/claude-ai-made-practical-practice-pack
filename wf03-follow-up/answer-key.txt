A follow-up email is not a fixed result, so this key lists what a good one contains and what it must not contain. Dates and amounts were checked with a script. Marcus's prices match his rate sheet in Workflow 19 (wf19-input-rate-sheet.txt).

PRACTICE RUN: Marcus Lee to Lucia Delgado, Hollis Street Bakery
(wf03-input-call-notes.txt and wf03-input-sent-email.txt)

Timeline facts
- Discovery call: Thursday, October 15, 2026. Marcus checked the pickup-day question on Friday, October 16.
- Proposal sent: Monday, October 19, 2026.
- Day of the follow-up: Tuesday, October 27, 2026 (8 days after the proposal).
- Next start date: Monday, November 30, 2026. Site plus online store: 8 to 10 weeks, so launch Monday, January 25 to Monday, February 8, 2027, if deposit, text and photos are in on November 30.
- Easter 2027: Sunday, March 28. Comfortably after the latest launch date.

Amounts (from the sent email; must not change)
- Build: $3,600 site + $1,200 online store = $4,800.
- Payments: $2,400 deposit, $1,200 at design approval, $1,200 at launch.
- Optional care plan: $85 a month, 12-month minimum = $1,020 for the first year. Build plus a year of care plan = $5,820.

Must include
- The answer to the pickup-day question: yes, the store setup's date picker can be set up as a pickup-date picker.
- Both example links, exactly as in the notes:
  https://coastlinekayak.example.com (customers pick a date and pay online, like choosing a pickup day)
  https://alderstreetdental.example.com (pages the office staff update themselves)
- One easy question at the end.
- A subject line.
- Under 120 words (under 100 after the repair request).

Must not include
- Any new price, discount, deadline or promise. In particular, no made-up deadline to "hold" the November 30 start date. The notes do not give one.
- A new or earlier launch date.
- "Just checking in," "following up," "I haven't heard back," or similar openers.
- A recap of the full proposal.

Things a good "sort out before sending" list catches
- Budget: the $4,800 build fits Lucia's "could go to 5," but the build plus a year of the care plan ($5,820) does not. She asked about skipping the care plan; it is optional.
- Neither example site is a bakery. Lucia asked for food or bakery examples.
- Teo (co-owner, signs off on anything over $2,000) has not been part of any conversation. Marcus offered a call with him.
- To keep the November 30 start, Marcus needs the deposit, text and photos by then. He has not said when he needs a decision.
