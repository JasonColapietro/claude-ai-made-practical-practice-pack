I uploaded [DOCUMENT: the lease for our new apartment in Denver]. Summarize it for [READER: the two of us], who have to decide [DECISION: whether to sign it as written].

Use only the uploaded document. After every point, put the section number it comes from in brackets, like [Section 4.2]. If you cannot point to a section, leave the point out.

Shape:
1. Overview: five lines covering who, what, how long, how much, and how it ends.
2. Key dates and deadlines: a table with Date or deadline, What happens, Section.
3. Money: every fee, charge and credit, with amounts, in a table.
4. What we must do and what [OTHER PARTY: the landlord] must do, as two short lists.
5. Things to watch: terms that could cost us money or catch us by surprise.
6. Problems in the document: sections that conflict with each other, or that refer to a section or addendum that does not exist. Quote the exact words.
7. Move-in costs: everything we must pay before we get the keys, with a total.

Keep it to about one page. Use plain English. If you use a legal term, explain it in a few words.

Check: Do not give legal advice. If a point needs a lawyer's opinion, say so in one line. If the document does not answer something a reader would expect, such as a start date or a price, say "Not stated."
