RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

Property: 2215 Larkspur Street, Unit 4B, Denver, Colorado 80207 (the "Premises")

Landlord: Larkspur Flats LLC, managed by Pinewood Row Property Management, LLC ("Landlord")
Landlord's office: 1450 Pinewood Row, Suite 210, Denver, Colorado 80205. Phone 303-555-0118. Email leasing@pinewoodrow.example.com

Tenants: Ada Okafor and Tunde Okafor ("Tenant," meaning each of them, jointly and individually)

Other approved occupants: Chidi Okafor and Zara Okafor (minors)

Date of this Lease: February 26, 2027


1. TERM

1.1 This Lease begins on April 15, 2027 and ends on April 14, 2028 (the "Term").

1.2 Keys will be released on the start date, or up to two (2) days earlier if Landlord agrees in writing, once Tenant has paid all Move-In Charges in Section 5 and provided proof of insurance under Section 12.


2. RENT

2.1 Monthly rent is $2,650.00, due on or before the first (1st) day of each month.

2.2 Rent is paid through Landlord's online resident portal, or by certified check or money order delivered to Landlord's office. Landlord does not accept cash.

2.3 Rent will not increase during the Term.


3. UTILITIES AND SERVICES

3.1 Tenant pays for electricity, natural gas and internet, and will put those accounts in Tenant's name before the start date.

3.2 Landlord provides water, sewer and trash service. Tenant pays a flat utility charge of $95.00 per month for these services, due with rent.

3.3 Landlord may change the flat utility charge once per year on sixty (60) days' written notice.


4. LATE PAYMENT AND RETURNED PAYMENTS

4.1 If Landlord has not received the full monthly rent by 11:59 p.m. on the seventh (7th) day of the month, Tenant owes a late fee of $50.00.

4.2 A payment returned by the bank for any reason carries a $25.00 returned payment fee. After two (2) returned payments, Landlord may require certified funds.


5. MOVE-IN CHARGES

Before keys are released, Tenant must pay:

   (a) the first month's rent, $2,650.00;
   (b) the first month's utility charge under Section 3.2, $95.00;
   (c) the security deposit under Section 6, $2,650.00; and
   (d) a one-time lease administration fee of $200.00, which is not refundable.


6. SECURITY DEPOSIT

6.1 The security deposit is $2,650.00. It is not a limit on what Tenant may owe.

6.2 Landlord will return the deposit, minus any lawful deductions, with a written list of those deductions, within thirty (30) days after Tenant moves out and returns all keys.

6.3 Landlord may deduct unpaid rent, unpaid charges, and the cost to repair damage beyond normal wear and tear. Normal wear and tear is not deducted.

6.4 Tenant must give Landlord a forwarding address in writing when moving out.


7. USE AND OCCUPANCY

7.1 Only the Tenants and the approved occupants listed above may live in the Premises. A guest may stay up to fourteen (14) nights in any six (6) month period without Landlord's written approval.

7.2 The Premises may be used only as a private home.


8. PARKING

8.1 Tenant is assigned one (1) parking space, Space 14, in the rear lot, included in the rent.

8.2 A second space may be rented for $75.00 per month under the Parking Addendum (Addendum C), if one is available.

8.3 Vehicles must be registered and in working order. Landlord may tow vehicles parked in another resident's space or in a fire lane, at the vehicle owner's cost.


9. PETS

No animals of any kind may be kept on the Premises, even temporarily, without a signed Pet Addendum. Service animals and assistance animals are allowed as required by law.


10. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

10.1 Tenant will keep the Premises clean and will report needed repairs through the resident portal promptly.

10.2 For emergencies such as flooding, no heat in cold weather, a gas smell or a fire, Tenant will call 911 if needed and then call Landlord's 24-hour emergency line at 303-555-0177.

10.3 Landlord will make repairs needed to keep the Premises safe and fit to live in. Tenant pays for damage caused by Tenant, occupants or guests beyond normal wear and tear.

10.4 Tenant will replace smoke detector batteries and furnace filters supplied by Landlord, and will not disable any smoke or carbon monoxide detector.


11. ALTERATIONS

Tenant may not paint, install shelves or fixtures, change locks or make other alterations without Landlord's written consent. Small nail holes for hanging pictures are allowed.


12. RENTERS INSURANCE

Tenant must carry renters insurance with at least $100,000 in personal liability coverage for the whole Term, and must give Landlord proof of coverage before keys are released. Landlord's insurance does not cover Tenant's belongings.


13. LANDLORD ENTRY

Landlord may enter the Premises at reasonable times to inspect, make repairs or show the unit, after giving at least twenty-four (24) hours' notice. Landlord may enter without notice in an emergency.


14. NOTICE TO MOVE OUT; RENEWAL

14.1 Either Tenant or Landlord may end this Lease at the end of the Term by giving written notice at least sixty (60) days before April 14, 2028.

14.2 If neither party gives that notice, this Lease continues month to month on the same terms, except that rent becomes $2,800.00 per month. After that, either party may end the month-to-month tenancy with at least twenty-one (21) days' written notice.


15. EARLY TERMINATION

15.1 Tenant may end this Lease before the end of the Term by (a) giving at least sixty (60) days' written notice, (b) paying rent through the notice period, and (c) paying an early termination fee equal to two (2) months' rent, $5,300.00.

15.2 This Section does not limit any rights Tenant has under law, including rights for military service members and for victims of domestic violence.


16. MOVE-OUT CONDITION

16.1 Tenant will return the Premises clean, with all trash removed and all keys, fobs and garage remotes returned.

16.2 Landlord and Tenant may do a walk-through together within three (3) days before move-out if Tenant requests one in writing.

16.3 Carpets must be professionally cleaned at move-out, and Tenant must provide the receipt. If Tenant does not, Landlord will have the carpets cleaned and deduct the actual cost from the deposit.


17. RULES

17.1 Quiet hours are 10:00 p.m. to 7:00 a.m. every day.

17.2 Smoking and vaping of any substance are not allowed anywhere on the property, including balconies and the parking lot.

17.3 Grills that use charcoal or open flame are not allowed on balconies. Electric grills are allowed.

17.4 Rent received after the fifth (5th) of the month is late and may be reported to the resident ledger as a late payment.

17.5 Bicycles may be stored in the basement bike room or inside the unit, not in hallways or stairwells.


18. MOVE-IN CONDITION REPORT

Within seven (7) days after the start date, Tenant will complete and return the move-in condition report provided by Landlord, noting any existing damage. Landlord will sign it and return a copy to Tenant.


19. NOTICES

Notices to Landlord must be in writing and sent through the resident portal or by mail to Landlord's office address above. Notices to Tenant may be sent through the resident portal, by email, or by delivery to the Premises.


20. ADDENDA

The following addenda are part of this Lease: Addendum A (Lead-Based Paint Disclosure) and Addendum B (Move-In Condition Report form).


21. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Lease and its addenda are the whole agreement between Landlord and Tenant. Any change must be in writing and signed by Landlord and all Tenants.
