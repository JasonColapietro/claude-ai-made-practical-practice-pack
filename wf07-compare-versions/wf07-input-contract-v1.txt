WEBSITE DESIGN SERVICES AGREEMENT
Version 1. Sent by Designer to Client on October 19, 2026.

This Website Design Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Marcus Lee, doing business as Marcus Lee Design ("Designer"), and Bramblewood Pottery Studio LLC ("Client"). Designer and Client are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

1. PARTIES AND EFFECTIVE DATE

1.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date the last Party signs it (the "Effective Date").

1.2 Client's main contact for this project is Claire Bennett, Owner, claire@bramblewoodpottery.example.com. Designer's contact is Marcus Lee, marcus@marcusleedesign.example.com. Either Party may change its contact by email.

2. SCOPE OF WORK

2.1 Designer will redesign and rebuild Client's public website (the "Project"). The new site will include the following 7 pages: Home, Classes, Open Studio, Gallery, About Us, Frequently Asked Questions, and Contact.

2.2 The site will be mobile responsive, meaning it will display and work properly on current versions of common phone, tablet and desktop browsers.

2.3 Designer will set up class sign-up with online payment on the Classes and Open Studio pages, using the sign-up and payment service Client chooses. Designer is not responsible for that service itself, its fees, or its outages.

2.4 Designer will complete basic on-page search setup for each page: page titles, meta descriptions, image alt text, and a submitted sitemap.

2.5 Designer will install a site analytics tool chosen by Client and confirm that it records visits. Designer will hold one 1-hour training call so Client can edit hours, banners, class listings and simple text.

2.6 The following are not included in the Project: writing new copy, photography or video, logo or brand identity work, an online store, migration of blog posts or other content from the current site, and ongoing maintenance after the support period in Section 8. Designer can quote any of these separately.

3. PROJECT TIMELINE

3.1 The Project start date is Monday, November 30, 2026. Designer will hold a 60-minute kickoff call with Client that week.

3.2 Designer will deliver the homepage design within 10 business days after receiving all Client Materials (defined in Section 4.1). Designer will deliver designs for the remaining page types within 10 business days after Client approves the homepage design.

3.3 Designer will deliver a working version of the full site on a private test address for Client review within 15 business days after Client approves all page designs.

3.4 The target launch date is January 22, 2027, provided Client meets the feedback deadlines in Section 4. If Client's feedback or materials arrive late, the launch date moves back by the same number of business days.

4. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

4.1 Client will provide all text, photos, logo files, class descriptions, class prices, and logins for the current hosting account and sign-up service ("Client Materials") by the Project start date.

4.2 Client will review each deliverable and send consolidated feedback within 5 business days after Designer delivers it.

4.3 Client is responsible for the accuracy of all Client Materials, including prices, class times, studio safety rules and policies shown on the site.

4.4 If Client feedback or Client Materials are delayed more than 15 business days past the deadlines above, the Project will be paused. When Client is ready to continue, Designer will set a new timeline based on Designer's availability at that time.

5. REVISIONS

5.1 The Project fee includes two (2) rounds of revisions on the page designs and one (1) round of revisions on the working site before launch.

5.2 A round means one consolidated list of changes sent by Client at one time. Separate emails sent on different days count as separate rounds unless Designer agrees otherwise.

5.3 Additional revisions, and any changes requested after Client approves a design, will be billed at Designer's hourly rate of $90 per hour. Designer will give Client a time estimate before starting additional revision work.

6. FEES AND PAYMENT

6.1 The total fee for the Project is $4,350 (the "Project Fee"). This is a 6-page redesign ($3,600), one additional page ($300), and class sign-up with online payment ($450).

6.2 Client will pay the Project Fee as follows:
(a) 50% ($2,175) as a deposit, due when Client signs this Agreement. The start date is held when the deposit is received.
(b) 25% ($1,087.50) due when Client approves all page designs.
(c) 25% ($1,087.50) due at launch.

6.3 Designer will send an invoice for each payment. Invoices are due within 15 days of the invoice date (net 15).

6.4 Any balance more than 30 days overdue will incur a late fee of 1.5% per month on the unpaid amount. Designer may pause work while any invoice is more than 30 days overdue.

6.5 Client may pay by bank transfer or card. Card payments include the card processor's fee.

6.6 Client pays its hosting, domain, and sign-up and payment service fees directly to those providers.

7. EXPENSES

7.1 Client will reimburse Designer for third-party costs that Client approves in advance, such as premium fonts, stock photos, or paid plugins. Designer will not buy any single item over $50 without Client's approval.

7.2 Designer will list approved expenses on the next invoice, with receipts.

8. HOSTING, LAUNCH AND SUPPORT

8.1 Client will keep its own hosting account and domain name and will pay for them directly. Designer will launch the site on Client's existing hosting account.

8.2 After launch, Designer will provide 30 days of support, at no extra charge, to fix bugs and errors in the work Designer delivered. Support does not include new features, new pages, or content updates.

8.3 After the support period, Client may choose Designer's optional care plan at $85 per month (12-month minimum) under a separate agreement.

9. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 Upon receipt of full payment of the Project Fee and any approved expenses, Client will own the final site design, the page layouts made for Client, and all Client Materials.

9.2 Designer keeps ownership of Designer's pre-existing tools, code snippets, templates and working methods that Designer used to build the site ("Designer Tools"). Designer grants Client a permanent, non-exclusive license to use the Designer Tools as part of Client's website.

9.3 Third-party items, such as fonts, stock photos and plugins, remain subject to their own license terms.

10. PORTFOLIO USE

10.1 Designer may display the completed site, screenshots of it, and a short description of the Project in Designer's portfolio and marketing materials.

10.2 The site footer will include a small credit link reading "Site by Marcus Lee Design." Client may ask Designer to remove the credit link at any time after 12 months from launch.

11. CONFIDENTIALITY

11.1 Each Party shall keep confidential any non-public business information it receives from the other Party, including student lists, sign-up data, pricing plans and login credentials.

11.2 Each Party shall use that information only to perform this Agreement and shall not share it with anyone except employees or contractors who need it for the Project.

11.3 These duties continue for two (2) years after this Agreement ends.

12. TERMINATION

12.1 Either Party may end this Agreement by giving the other Party fourteen (14) days' written notice by email.

12.2 If Client ends this Agreement, Client will pay for all work completed up to the end date. The deposit is non-refundable.

12.3 If Designer ends this Agreement for a reason other than Client's breach, Designer will refund any amount paid for work not yet started and will deliver all completed work to Client.

12.4 Sections 9, 11, 13 and 14 continue after this Agreement ends.

13. WARRANTIES AND LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

13.1 Designer warrants that the work will be performed in a professional manner and that the original design work will not knowingly copy anyone else's work.

13.2 Except as stated in Section 13.1, the work is provided "as is." Designer does not guarantee any particular search ranking, number of visitors, or number of class sign-ups.

13.3 Designer's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the total fees Client has paid to Designer under this Agreement.

14. INDEMNIFICATION

14.1 Each Party will defend and hold harmless the other Party against third-party claims arising from materials that the first Party provided. For Client, this includes Client Materials. For Designer, this includes the original design work.

15. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR

15.1 Designer is an independent contractor, not an employee of Client. Designer controls how and when the work is done, provides Designer's own equipment, and is responsible for Designer's own taxes.

16. GOVERNING LAW

16.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Designer's principal place of business is located.

16.2 The Parties will try in good faith to resolve any dispute by talking first, before taking any formal action.

17. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

17.1 This Agreement is the complete agreement between the Parties about the Project and replaces any earlier proposals or discussions.

17.2 Any change to this Agreement must be in writing and accepted by both Parties. An email that both Parties reply to with "agreed" counts as writing.
