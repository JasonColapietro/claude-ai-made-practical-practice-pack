NORTHFIELD PHYSICAL THERAPY
Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
Policy HR-07. Effective January 1, 2027. Applies to all three clinics.

1. PURPOSE

1.1 This policy explains how team members earn, request and use paid time off (PTO). PTO covers vacation and personal days. Sick time is now handled separately (see Section 5).

2. WHO IS ELIGIBLE

2.1 All full-time and part-time team members are eligible for PTO starting on their first day of work. Per-diem and temporary staff are not eligible.

2.2 Team members may use PTO after completing 90 days of employment. PTO earned during the first 90 days stays in the team member's bank.

3. HOW PTO IS EARNED

3.1 Full-time team members (scheduled 36 or more hours a week) earn PTO each pay period based on length of service:
(a) Less than 3 years of service: 4.62 hours per pay period, about 120 hours (15 days) a year.
(b) 3 or more years of service: 6.15 hours per pay period, about 160 hours (20 days) a year.

3.2 Part-time team members earn 1 hour of PTO for every 30 hours worked.

3.3 PTO does not accrue on overtime hours or during unpaid leave.

4. REQUESTING PTO

4.1 Team members submit PTO requests through the scheduling system.

4.2 Requests for 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 3 weeks before the first day off. Requests for 1 or 2 days should be submitted at least 48 hours ahead when possible.

4.3 Requests are approved by the team member's clinic manager. Requests for 5 or more consecutive days also need approval from the Operations Coordinator. Managers respond within 5 business days.

4.4 When two or more team members at the same clinic request the same dates, requests are approved in the order they were received.

4.5 From December 15 to December 31, no more than two team members per clinic may be on PTO on the same day. Requests for these dates received by October 1 are reviewed together. Requests received after October 1 are approved in the order received, if space remains.

5. SICK TIME AND UNPLANNED ABSENCES

5.1 Each team member receives 24 hours of paid sick time on January 1 of each year (prorated for team members hired during the year). Sick time does not carry over and is not paid out when a team member leaves.

5.2 Team members who are sick or have an emergency must call or text their clinic manager at least 2 hours before their shift starts.

5.3 Unplanned absences use sick time first, then PTO. If a team member has no sick time or PTO left, the absence is unpaid.

5.4 After 3 or more consecutive sick days, the manager may ask for a note from a health care provider.

6. CARRYOVER

6.1 Team members may carry over up to 24 hours of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Hours above 24 are forfeited on January 1.

7. PAID HOLIDAYS

7.1 The clinics are closed on six paid holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. These days do not use PTO.

7.2 Team members who are scheduled to work on a holiday because of a special clinic schedule receive a substitute day off within 30 days.

8. PTO WHEN LEAVING NORTHFIELD

8.1 Team members who resign with at least 2 weeks' notice are paid for unused PTO in their final paycheck, up to a maximum of 40 hours.

8.2 Team members who resign with less than 2 weeks' notice, or who are dismissed for cause, are not paid for unused PTO.

9. QUESTIONS

9.1 Team members should direct questions about this policy to their clinic manager or to the Operations Coordinator, Priya Shah.
