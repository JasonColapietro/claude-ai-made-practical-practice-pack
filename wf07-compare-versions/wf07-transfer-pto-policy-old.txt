NORTHFIELD PHYSICAL THERAPY
Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy
Policy HR-07. Effective January 1, 2025. Applies to all three clinics.

1. PURPOSE

1.1 This policy explains how employees earn, request and use paid time off (PTO). PTO covers vacation, personal days and sick days in one bank of hours.

2. WHO IS ELIGIBLE

2.1 All full-time and part-time employees are eligible for PTO starting on their first day of work. Per-diem and temporary staff are not eligible.

2.2 Employees may use PTO after completing 60 days of employment. PTO earned during the first 60 days stays in the employee's bank.

3. HOW PTO IS EARNED

3.1 Full-time employees (scheduled 36 or more hours a week) earn 4.62 hours of PTO each pay period. This equals about 120 hours (15 days) a year.

3.2 Part-time employees earn PTO in proportion to the hours they work, at the same rate as full-time employees.

3.3 PTO does not accrue on overtime hours or during unpaid leave.

4. REQUESTING PTO

4.1 Employees submit PTO requests through the scheduling system.

4.2 Requests for 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks before the first day off. Requests for 1 or 2 days should be submitted at least 48 hours ahead when possible.

4.3 Requests are approved by the employee's clinic manager. Managers respond within 5 business days.

4.4 When two or more employees at the same clinic request the same dates, requests are approved in the order they were received.

5. UNPLANNED ABSENCES

5.1 Employees who are sick or have an emergency must call their clinic manager at least 1 hour before their shift starts. A text message is not enough.

5.2 PTO is used for unplanned absences. If an employee has no PTO left, the absence is unpaid.

5.3 After 3 or more consecutive sick days, the manager may ask for a note from a health care provider.

6. CARRYOVER

6.1 Employees may carry over up to 40 hours of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Hours above 40 are forfeited on January 1.

7. PAID HOLIDAYS

7.1 The clinics are closed on six paid holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day and Christmas Day. These days do not use PTO.

7.2 Employees who are scheduled to work on a holiday because of a special clinic schedule receive a substitute day off within 30 days.

8. PTO WHEN LEAVING NORTHFIELD

8.1 Employees who resign with at least 2 weeks' notice are paid for all unused PTO in their final paycheck.

8.2 Employees who resign with less than 2 weeks' notice, or who are dismissed for cause, are not paid for unused PTO.

9. QUESTIONS

9.1 Employees should direct questions about this policy to their clinic manager or to the Operations Coordinator, Priya Shah.
