MAPLE STREET COMMUNITY GARDEN: RAIN BARREL QUESTION
Notes from Lena Brooks, coordinator. For the committee meeting.

THE IDEA
At the last committee meeting, Walt proposed we put rain barrels on the tool shed to cut our water bill. The shed has a metal roof and one gutter on the back side. Walt thinks four 55-gallon barrels linked together would fit along that wall. Our water comes from one city spigot, and we paid for every gallon last summer.

ABOUT US
- Maple Street Community Garden, Columbus, Ohio. 40 plots, all volunteer run.
- The lot belongs to a neighborhood nonprofit. We have a written use agreement with them. (Does it say anything about adding structures? I need to check. The agreement is in the shared folder.)
- Most plots grow vegetables and herbs. Some families have young kids who help water.

WHAT WE WANT TO KNOW
1. Is it legal to collect rainwater in Ohio? Someone at the meeting said it's illegal in some states and she thought Ohio was one of them.
2. Does the City of Columbus require a permit or have rules for rain barrels, especially on a shared or nonprofit lot?
3. Is roof runoff from a metal shed roof safe to use on vegetables? Any official guidance on how to use it (watering the soil vs. the leaves, washing produce, etc.)?
4. What do we have to do about mosquitoes? I remember something about the city and standing water.
5. Are there any programs that give rain barrels to community gardens or help pay for them?
6. What do we do with the barrels in winter?

We'd like an answer we can hand out at the meeting, with links people can check.
