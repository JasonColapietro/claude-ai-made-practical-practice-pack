## 2. Monthly cost (5 days a week, 20 school days)

**Pinecrest:** $95 a week × 4 weeks = **$380.00 a month**. One-time registration $35 (once per school year), so the first month is $415.00.

**Bright Peaks:** the flyer says **$520.00 a month**. The FAQ says $135 a week; × 4 weeks = **$540.00 a month**. Registration is $75 per family, one time. First month: $595.00 at the flyer price, $615.00 at the FAQ price.

**Trailhead:** $28 a day × 20 days = **$560.00 a month**. Registration $40, so the first month is $600.00 (not under $600). Early-release days cost $15 extra each; the number of those days isn't in the files, so I left them out.

## 4. Problems

**Contradictions**
- Bright Peaks lists two full-time prices: $520 a month on the flyer, $135 a week on the FAQ ($540 for 4 weeks). That's a $20 a month difference.

**Conditions attached to prices**
- Bright Peaks: "Enroll any Monday, space permitting." Holiday Camp is $65 a day.
- Trailhead: minimum 3 days a week; early-release days are $15 extra.
- Pinecrest: registration is per school year, so you may pay it again in the fall.

**Things your needs file asks that the programs don't answer**
- Holidays and early release are answered: Pinecrest is closed on both. Bright Peaks runs Holiday Camp on most holidays ($65 a day) and covers early release at no charge. Trailhead is closed on holidays and covers early release for $15.
- None of the three says whether they want the registration fee before a spot is confirmed. (Pinecrest's waitlist itself is free.)
- Pickup timing at Pinecrest: you get off at 5:30 and need about 25 minutes, so you'd arrive around 5:55 for a 6:00 close. None of the files mention a late-pickup policy.

## 5. Bottom line

Trailhead is out because it closes at 5:45 p.m., even though it has a spot and is at the school. Bright Peaks meets all four must-haves as written, at $520 or $540 a month, but you need to confirm the price and an April 5 start. Pinecrest is the cheapest at $380 a month and meets the hours and travel must-haves, but it is full with a waitlist of 9 families. Joining the waitlist is free, so you could do that while you confirm Bright Peaks.
