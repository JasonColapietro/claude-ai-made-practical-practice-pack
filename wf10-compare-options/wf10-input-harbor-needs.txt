HARBOR & HOME CLEANING: WHAT WE NEED FROM A SUPPLY SUPPLIER
Written by Dana Ruiz, October 2026

Why we're switching
Right now I buy supplies at a big-box store twice a month and haul them back in my car. It eats most of a Saturday and the store keeps running out of the fragrance-free gallons. I asked three suppliers for proposals. I want to pick one before the end of November so the new orders start in December.

How we order
One order a month, placed the last week of the month. Delivered to our office: 214 Fore Street, Suite 3, Portland, Maine. Someone is at the office Monday to Friday, 8 to 5. No weekend deliveries.

Our usual monthly order (7 cleaners)
- All-purpose cleaner concentrate, 1-gallon: 8
- Glass cleaner concentrate, 1-gallon: 4
- Bathroom cleaner (tub, tile, toilet), 1-gallon: 6
- Floor cleaner concentrate, 1-gallon: 3
- Microfiber cloths, pack of 12: 10 packs
- Nitrile gloves, size M and L, box of 100: 10 boxes

Must-haves (a supplier that misses one of these is out)
1. Fragrance-free versions of ALL four cleaners. Our default products are fragrance-free and clients count on that.
2. Pet-safe products available when a client asks for them. They don't all have to be pet-safe, we just need the option.
3. Delivered within 5 business days of ordering.
4. No contract that locks us in for more than 6 months. Month-to-month is best.

Budget
Keep the full monthly order, delivery included, under $700 a month.

Nice-to-haves (break a tie)
- Net 30 payment terms. Paying by card at the time of order is OK but not my favorite.
- A real person I can call when something is wrong with an order.
- Concentrates or refills so we throw away fewer plastic jugs.
- One supplier for everything, including gloves and cloths.

Things I'm not sure about
- Ellie (one of our cleaners) says one of these companies' bathroom cleaners gave her a headache at her last job. She can't remember which one.
- I think we used about 9 gallons of all-purpose in September but that was a busy month. 8 is normal.
