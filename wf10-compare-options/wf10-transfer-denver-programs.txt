THREE AFTER-SCHOOL PROGRAMS NEAR ASPEN HOLLOW ELEMENTARY
(Copied by Ada from each program's website and emails, October 2026)

==========================================================
1. PINECREST REC CENTER KIDS CLUB
==========================================================
From the city recreation center website:

Kids Club is a supervised after-school program for kids in grades K to 5. Activities include gym games, crafts, outdoor play (weather permitting), and a quiet homework hour from 4:00 to 5:00 p.m.

Hours: School days, dismissal to 6:00 p.m.
Location: Pinecrest Recreation Center, 0.4 miles from Aspen Hollow Elementary.
Getting there: Staff walk kids from Aspen Hollow, Lakeview and Garfield Park elementaries to the center each day.
Cost: $95 per week (5 days). Residents and non-residents pay the same rate for Kids Club.
Registration fee: $35 per child, once per school year.
Staff ratio: 1 adult for every 12 children.

From an email Ada got on October 14:
"Hi Ada, thanks for reaching out. Kids Club is full for the rest of this school year and we have a waitlist of 9 families. Spots usually open up in March when families move or change schedules. I'd suggest getting on the waitlist now. There's no fee to join the waitlist.
Marisol, Youth Programs"

Closed: school holidays, and the rec center does not run Kids Club on early-release days.

==========================================================
2. BRIGHT PEAKS LEARNING LAB
==========================================================
From the Bright Peaks flyer:

Where curious kids build, code and create. Bright Peaks runs hands-on art and STEM projects every afternoon, plus a 30-minute homework block with a certified teacher on staff.

Hours: 3:00 to 6:30 p.m., Monday through Friday.
Pickup: Our vans collect students from 6 partner schools: Aspen Hollow, Lakeview, Garfield Park, Ridge Road, St. Anne's and Westgate.
Tuition: $520 per month, 5 days a week. 3-day option: $340 per month.
Registration: $75 per family, one time.
Enroll any Monday, space permitting.

From the Bright Peaks FAQ page:
"How much does Bright Peaks cost? Full-time tuition is $135 per week. Part-time (3 days) is $90 per week. We bill monthly."
"What about school holidays? We offer full-day Holiday Camp on most school holidays for $65 per day. Early-release days are covered at no extra charge."
"Do you have openings? We currently have space in our 3rd to 5th grade group."

3. TRAILHEAD KIDS ADVENTURE CLUB
==========================================================
From an email from the director, Josh, October 20:

"Hi Ada and Tunde,

Great to hear from you. Trailhead runs right at Aspen Hollow, so Zara would just walk down to the gym after the last bell. We spend most afternoons outside (hikes in the park, nature journaling, building forts), and on bad-weather days we do art and games inside.

We run until 5:45 p.m. every school day. Pricing is $28 per day, and we ask families to sign up for at least 3 days a week. There's a $40 registration fee when you enroll. We do have room for a 4th grader starting in April.

We don't run on school holidays, but we do cover early-release days for an extra $15.

Homework time is optional. Kids can do it at the picnic tables if they want to, but it's not a structured block.

Let me know if you want to come see it. We're there every afternoon.

Josh
Trailhead Kids Adventure Club"
