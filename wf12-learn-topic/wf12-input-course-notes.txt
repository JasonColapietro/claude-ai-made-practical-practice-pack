Debits and credits (Sam's notes from class + chapter 3)

THE BIG RULE
Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity
Every transaction keeps this balanced. Every transaction touches at least 2 accounts.

Debit = left side. Credit = right side. That's ALL it means. (Instructor said this 3 times.)
Total debits must equal total credits for every entry.

T-ACCOUNT
        Cash
  Debit   |  Credit
  (left)  |  (right)

ACCOUNT TYPES AND WHICH SIDE MAKES THEM GO UP
(normal balance = the side that increases it)

  Assets (Cash, Accounts Receivable, Supplies, Equipment)   -> Debit
  Liabilities (Accounts Payable, Loans)                     -> Credit
  Owner's Capital                                           -> Credit
  Revenue (Service Revenue)                                 -> Credit
  Expenses (Rent Expense, Utilities Expense)                -> Debit
  Owner's Drawing                                           -> Credit

To DECREASE an account, use the opposite side.

Memory trick from the instructor: DEALER
  Drawings, Expenses, Assets = go up with a Debit
  Liabilities, Equity, Revenue = go up with a Credit

WORDS
- "On account" = no cash changes hands yet. Buying on account = we owe it (Accounts Payable). Selling on account = they owe us (Accounts Receivable).
- Revenue is recorded when the work is DONE, not when the money comes in.
- Owner's Drawing = the owner taking money out for personal use. Not an expense of the business.
