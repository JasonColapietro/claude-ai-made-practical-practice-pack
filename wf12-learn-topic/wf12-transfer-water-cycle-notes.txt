KEY IDEAS
- The water cycle is the continuous movement of water between Earth's surface and the atmosphere.
- The total amount of water on Earth stays about the same. It changes state (solid, liquid, gas) and changes place.
- Energy from the sun drives the cycle. Gravity pulls water back down and downhill.

  Chidi: sun = engine. gravity = brings it back.

THE PROCESSES
Evaporation: liquid water turns into water vapor (a gas) when it is heated. Most evaporation is from the oceans.

  Chidi: puddles disappearing. faster when hot.

Transpiration: plants take up water through their roots and release water vapor through tiny openings in their leaves.

  Chidi: plants "sweating"?? kind of

Condensation: water vapor in the air cools and turns into water.

  Chidi: condensation = when the water vapor turns into rain. clouds = water vapor.

Precipitation: water falls from clouds to the ground as rain, snow, sleet or hail when the droplets or ice crystals get too heavy.

Collection, runoff and infiltration: water collects in oceans, lakes and rivers. Runoff is water that flows over the land into streams and rivers. Infiltration is water soaking into the ground, where it can become groundwater.

  Chidi: infiltration = into. runoff = runs off. easy

Sublimation: ice or snow turns directly into water vapor without melting first. Happens on cold, dry, sunny days and high on mountains.
