# Frequently asked questions

## Prices and discounts

**How much does a regular cleaning cost?**
A standard home clean starts at $140 for homes up to 1,500 square feet. Larger homes add $25 for each extra 500 square feet. [Source: SERVICES AND PRICES]

**Is there a discount if I book every week?**
Yes. Standard cleans booked weekly or every two weeks are 10% off. [Source: SERVICES AND PRICES]

**Is there a minimum? I only need a small part of my home cleaned.**
Our minimum visit is $120. [Source: SERVICES AND PRICES]

**What's in a deep clean?**
A deep clean includes the inside of the oven, baseboards and interior windows. It starts at $260 for homes up to 1,500 square feet. [Source: SERVICES AND PRICES]

**Can you fold laundry while you're here?**
Yes, laundry folding is an add-on at $25 per load. [Source: SERVICES AND PRICES]

## Where we clean

**Which towns do you serve?**
We clean in Portland, South Portland, Westbrook, Falmouth and Cape Elizabeth. [Source: SERVICE AREA]

**Is Scarborough too far for you?**
We come to Scarborough and Yarmouth by request, with a $15 travel fee per visit. [Source: SERVICE AREA]

**Do you come out to Peaks Island?**
No. We don't provide service on the islands. [Source: SERVICE AREA]

## Booking, payment and changes

**How do I book my first cleaning?**
Call us at 207-555-0142 or email hello@harborhome.example.com. [Source: HOW BOOKING WORKS; contact details from the top of the facts file]

**I have a big house. How does a first clean work?**
For homes over 2,500 square feet, the first visit starts with a free 15-minute video walk-through. [Source: HOW BOOKING WORKS]

**Can I pay in cash?**
No, we don't take cash. You pay by card or bank transfer after each visit. [Source: HOW BOOKING WORKS]

**Is there a charge if I cancel?**
Cancelling is free up to 48 hours before your visit. Inside 48 hours, there's a $50 fee. [Source: HOW BOOKING WORKS]

**What happens if I forget to unlock the door?**
If our team can't get in, the visit is charged at the full price. [Source: HOW BOOKING WORKS]

**Can I come by your office?**
Our office is at 214 Fore Street, Suite 3, but we don't offer walk-in service. [Source: top of the facts file]

## Moving and offices

**We're moving out of our apartment. Do you do move-out cleans?**
Yes. A move-out or move-in clean starts at $320 for homes up to 1,500 square feet, and the home needs to be empty. Starting March 1, renters can also book our move-out package: the move-out clean plus the inside of the fridge and cabinets for $380, for homes up to 1,500 square feet. [Source: SERVICES AND PRICES; NEW IN SPRING]

**Do you clean offices? Can you come during the day?**
We clean small offices in the evenings only, after 5:30 p.m. We give you a price after a free walk-through. [Source: SERVICES AND PRICES]

## What we do and don't clean

**Do you shampoo carpets or rugs?**
No, we don't do carpet shampooing. [Source: WHAT WE DO NOT DO]

**Do you clean the outside of windows?**
No. We don't clean exterior windows. A deep clean does include interior windows. [Source: WHAT WE DO NOT DO; SERVICES AND PRICES]

**Can you dust the top of our kitchen cabinets? They're about 9 feet up.**
We don't do any work above 8 feet on a ladder. [Source: WHAT WE DO NOT DO]

**Can you clean the mildew on our bathroom ceiling?**
We don't do mold remediation. [Source: WHAT WE DO NOT DO]

## Supplies, your team and our guarantee

**Do you bring your own supplies?**
Yes. Our team brings all supplies and equipment. [Source: SUPPLIES]

**Someone in my home has allergies. Can you use our products?**
Yes, you're welcome to supply your own products. Our default products are fragrance-free. [Source: SUPPLIES]

**Are your products safe for pets?**
We use pet-safe products on request, so let us know when you book. [Source: SUPPLIES]

**Are you insured?**
Yes, we're insured and bonded, with general liability and bonding. We can send a certificate on request. [Source: top of the facts file]

**Are your cleaners employees, or people you hire off an app?**
All 7 of our cleaners are our own employees, not contractors. [Source: top of the facts file]

**What if you missed something?**
Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back and fix it at no charge. [Source: GUARANTEE]
