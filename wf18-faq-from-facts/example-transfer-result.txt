Here's a new-patient FAQ built only from the front desk facts sheet. Parking and cash differ by clinic, so I answered those clinic by clinic. Medical questions are in their own section at the end, unanswered.

# New patient FAQ

## Booking your first visit

**Can I just book online?**
Not for your first visit. New patients book by phone at 614-555-0180. Online booking is for returning patients only. [Source: SCHEDULING]

**How long is the first appointment?**
Plan on about 60 minutes for your first visit, which is your evaluation. Follow-up visits take about 45 minutes. [Source: SCHEDULING]

**How early should I get there? The email said 15 minutes.**
Please arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit, or 20 minutes early if you're filling out paper forms. The confirmation email still says 15 minutes, but 10 is correct. [Source: SCHEDULING]

**If I start at one clinic, can I switch to another later?**
Yes. You can be seen at any of our three clinics, and you can switch clinics later. [Source: SCHEDULING]

**Are you open Saturday?**
Our Westerville clinic is open Saturday, 8 a.m. to noon. Dublin and Grove City are open Monday to Friday. [Source: HOURS]

## Forms and what to bring

**Can I fill out the forms when I get there?**
Yes. Paper forms are available at the front desk. If you use them, please arrive 20 minutes early. [Source: FORMS AND WHAT TO BRING; SCHEDULING]

**What should I wear?**
Wear or bring clothes you can move in comfortably. Shorts are helpful if you're coming in for a knee, hip or ankle. [Source: FORMS AND WHAT TO BRING]

**Do I need a referral from my doctor?**
Bring a referral or prescription if your insurance requires one. Our front desk checks your benefits before your first visit. [Source: FORMS AND WHAT TO BRING; INSURANCE AND PAYMENT]

## Insurance and payment

**Do you take my insurance?**
We bill your insurance for you. Before your first visit, the front desk checks your benefits and tells you what you can expect to owe. [Source: INSURANCE AND PAYMENT]

**How much is it if I don't have insurance?**
The self-pay rate is $150 for the evaluation and $110 for each follow-up visit. [Source: INSURANCE AND PAYMENT]

**Can I pay cash?**
Cash is accepted at Dublin and Westerville, but not at Grove City. All three clinics take cards and checks. [Source: INSURANCE AND PAYMENT]

## Parking and getting in

**Where do I park?**
- Dublin: free parking in the front lot, with overflow parking behind the building.
- Westerville: free parking in the lot we share with the bank. Use a space marked "Northfield PT" or the rows farthest from the bank.
- Grove City: free parking in the shopping-center lot, with two accessible spaces right outside our door.
[Source: OUR CLINICS]

**Is the Westerville office wheelchair accessible? It's upstairs, right?**
Yes. Westerville is on the 2nd floor, with an elevator just inside the main door. All three clinics have step-free access. [Source: OUR CLINICS; LANGUAGE AND ACCESS]

**Is there someone who speaks Spanish?**
Phone interpreters are available at no charge. Just ask when you book. [Source: LANGUAGE AND ACCESS]
