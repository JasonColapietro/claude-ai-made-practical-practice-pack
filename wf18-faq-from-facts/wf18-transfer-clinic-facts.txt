SCHEDULING
- New patients book by phone. Online booking is for returning patients only.
- First visit (evaluation): plan on about 60 minutes. Follow-up visits: about 45 minutes.
- You can be seen at any of our three clinics, and you can switch clinics later.
- Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead. Late cancellations (less than 24 hours) and missed visits: $40 fee.
- Arrive 10 minutes early for your first visit, or 20 minutes early if you are filling out paper forms.
  (Note for staff: the booking confirmation email still says "15 minutes early." IT is fixing it. 10 is correct.)

FORMS AND WHAT TO BRING
- After you book, we email you a link to the new-patient forms. Please finish them at least 24 hours before your first visit.
- Paper forms are available at the front desk if you prefer.
- Bring: a photo ID, your insurance card, and a referral or prescription if your insurance requires one.
- Also bring a list of the medicines you take now (names and doses).
- Wear or bring clothes you can move in comfortably. Shorts are helpful if you are coming in for a knee, hip or ankle.

INSURANCE AND PAYMENT
- We bill your insurance for you. Before your first visit, the front desk checks your benefits and tells you what you can expect to owe.
- Copays are collected at check-in.
- We accept cards and checks at all three clinics. Cash is accepted at Dublin and Westerville only.
- Self-pay rates (no insurance): evaluation $150, follow-up visit $110.

LANGUAGE AND ACCESS
- Phone interpreters are available at no charge. Ask when you book.
- All three clinics have step-free access (Westerville by elevator).
