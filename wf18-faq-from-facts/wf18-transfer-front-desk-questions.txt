WHAT NEW PATIENTS ASK THE FRONT DESK
Collected by the three front desk teams, September to October 2026. Wording is as close to the caller's as we could get. No patient names or details.

DUBLIN
- "Where do I park?"
- "How long is the first appointment?"
- "Can I just book online?"
- "Is it OK to take my pain medicine before I come in?"
- "What should I wear?"
- "Can I bring my kid with me? I don't have a sitter."
- "How much is it if I don't have insurance?"

WESTERVILLE
- "Where do I park? The bank lot says customers only."
- "Are you open Saturday?"
- "Is the office wheelchair accessible? It's upstairs, right?"
- "Should I use ice or heat on my knee before the evaluation?"
- "Do I need a referral from my doctor?"
- "Can I pay cash?"
- "What happens if I'm running a few minutes late?"

GROVE CITY
- "Do you take my insurance? I have a plan through my husband's work."
- "Can I pay cash?"
- "Can I fill out the forms when I get there?"
- "Will it hurt?"
- "How many visits will I need?"
- "If I start here, can I switch to Dublin later? It's closer to work."
- "Is there someone who speaks Spanish?"
- "How early should I get there? The email said 15 minutes."
