Practice questions (chapter 8)
1. After adjusting entries are posted, what does the adjusted trial balance show, and why is it prepared before the financial statements?
2. Which financial statement is prepared first from the adjusted trial balance, and why does the next statement depend on it?
3. An adjusted trial balance shows Service Revenue $9,000, Rent Expense $1,200, Wages Expense $3,000 and Supplies Expense $300. What is net income?

Answers
1. It lists every account balance after the adjustments, and total debits should equal total credits. It's prepared first so the statements use up-to-date balances.
2. The income statement. Its net income (or loss) flows into the statement of owner's equity, which then feeds the balance sheet.
3. $9,000 minus $4,500 in expenses = $4,500 net income.
